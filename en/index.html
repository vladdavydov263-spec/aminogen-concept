Traceability

How a vial earns its certificate.

Four steps, one chain of documents. If any link is missing, the lot does not ship.

  1. 01

    Synthesis

    The peptide API is produced at a partner facility under documented quality systems. The lot number is assigned before filling.

  2. 02

    Filling and labelling

    Lyophilised powder in 3 ml vials — powder and water for dilution. Lot number, product identifier and QR code are printed on the box.

  3. 03

    Independent testing

    Janoshik Analytical (CZ) determines purity by HPLC, identity by LC-MS and content in the lot — not in a catalog sample.

  4. 04

    Release

    The report is matched to the lot number, published here and enclosed with the order. Temperature during transport is recorded.

Quality

What we publish —
and what we do not claim.

A purity figure without a report is marketing. Our position is simple: publish the document we actually received, name the laboratory, show the date — and let the numbers speak for themselves.

  • We publish reports for every lot, not one “typical” certificate.
  • We name the laboratory and the method used for testing.
  • We do not call a product GMP-certified unless the facility holds such a certificate for this exact product.
  • We do not sell, test or describe these compounds for human use.
