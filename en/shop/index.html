Ask a question
What exactly is in the box?

Two vials: the lyophilised powder in the stated amount and bacteriostatic water for reconstitution. The label carries the lot number and product identifier, and a certificate of analysis is provided for the lot.

Can I see the analytical report before ordering?

Yes. Every lot has a public report: enter the lot number in the checker or ask us for the report for the lot currently in stock. We do not send a generic certificate from another lot.

How fast is delivery?

Dispatch within 1–3 business days; delivery across Ukraine usually 1–3 business days with tracking. Delivery is free from 5 000 ₴, otherwise 200 ₴.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa and Mastercard, or a bank transfer to a sole-trader account. Payment in hryvnia.

Is this a medicine?

No. All products are supplied solely for laboratory research and analytical use. They are not medicinal products, are not intended for humans or animals, and we do not provide dosing or administration instructions.

What should I do if the parcel is damaged?

Photograph the packaging and contact us within 5 business days. If the lot or the documentation does not match your order, we will replace the goods or issue a credit note.
