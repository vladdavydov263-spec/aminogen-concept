How it works

Three steps, all online.

The provider can decline at any stage — that is precisely the point of their involvement.

01

Complete the assessment

About two minutes: your goal, height and weight, medical history and current medication. Nothing is shared with anyone until a provider reviews the assessment.

02

A provider reviews your assessment

A licensed provider checks whether treatment is appropriate for you. If it is not, you are not charged, and we explain the reason in plain language.

03

Prescription and delivery

If medication is prescribed, the prescription goes to the partner pharmacy and the medication is delivered to your home. Follow-ups continue for as long as you remain in the programme.

Medical information

Prescription treatment is prescribed only when a licensed provider, having reviewed your medical history, considers it clinically appropriate. The programme does not suit everyone: pregnancy and breastfeeding, certain thyroid and pancreatic conditions, and some other diagnoses are reasons why a provider will decline. Medication is dispensed by a licensed pharmacy against a prescription; it is not supplied for cosmetic use. The information on this page is general and is not medical advice; results vary and no outcome is guaranteed. If you are concerned about symptoms, contact your provider or emergency services.

Ask a question
Who can join the programme?

Adults (18+) living in a country where our provider network operates. The assessment asks about medical history because treatment depends on it — a provider may decline if a prescription would be unsafe for you.

Is this a subscription?

You choose monthly billing or a 3- or 6-month prepayment. The monthly plan can be cancelled at any time; a prepayment is refunded on a pro-rata basis if you stop for medical reasons.

What happens if I do not qualify?

You are not charged, and you receive a short explanation. No prescription is issued and no medication is dispensed.

Where does the medication come from?

From a licensed pharmacy in Ukraine — against a prescription issued by your provider. Within this programme we do not send research materials; the research shop is a separate service.

How fast is delivery?

Once the prescription is issued, dispensing usually takes 1–2 business days, and delivery across Ukraine 1–3 business days, with tracking.

What happens to my medical data?

It is accessible to the provider reviewing your assessment and to the pharmacy dispensing your prescription. It is not used for advertising and not sold. You can request access or deletion at any time.
